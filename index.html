Our Charitable Purpose

Registered to Advance Community

Maufant Youth Project is registered with the Jersey Charity Commissioner for the advancement of citizenship or community development.

01

Youth Work Opportunities

To support the development of youth work opportunities for young people living in Jersey — whatever their background.

02

Personal & Social Growth

To promote their personal and social development, building confidence, resilience and friendships that last beyond the club.

03

A Voice That's Heard

To enable young people to have a voice which is heard — in their local parish and across the Island of Jersey.

The income and property of the charity are applied solely towards the promotion of these objects.

Our Story

Built by the Parish, for the Parish

The Maufant Youth Project grew out of a simple local question: what is there for young people in the east of the Island once the school day ends? From that question, a volunteer committee, a set of keys to the Centre, and a lot of evenings spent listening to teenagers.

Today we are a registered Jersey charity (No. 340, AJC 505) run by local trustees. We work in partnership with families, schools and parishes — and the Centre is also used by the wider community, from polling days to open days.

Where Your Money Goes

Transparent to the last pound

Every pound given to Maufant Youth Project is spent on young people in the east of the Island. We hold no reserves beyond one term's running costs, and our accounts are filed with the Jersey Charity Commissioner as required.

  • No paid staff — trustees and session leaders are all volunteers
