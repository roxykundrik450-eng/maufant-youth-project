Our Mission

Three Objects, One Purpose

These are the charitable objects as registered with the Jersey Charity Commissioner. Everything we do has to trace back to one of them.

01

Support youth work opportunities

To support the development of youth work opportunities for young people, so that age, income or postcode never decides who gets to join in.

02

Promote personal and social development

To promote young people's personal and social development — confidence, teamwork, new skills and the resilience to navigate the years ahead.

03

Enable a voice that's heard

To enable young people to have a voice which is heard in their local parish and in the Island of Jersey — not tokenistically, but in the rooms where decisions are made.

"The income and property of the charity are applied solely towards the promotion of the objects."

How We Work

The Principles That Guide Us

Free to Attend

A young person's ability to pay should never be the reason they miss out. Sessions are free, and we quietly cover costs where it matters.

Local & Accountable

We are Jersey-registered, volunteer-run and answerable to our members, their families and our parish. Our accounts are filed as required.

Led by Young People

Members help shape the programme, plan the trips and speak up about what the Project should be doing next. It's their club.

Our Story So Far

A Jersey Project, Year by Year

The Beginning

A club with nowhere to go

Local families and volunteers start running informal youth sessions in the east of the Island, using whatever space they can borrow.

The Centre Opens

A permanent home at Maufant

The Maufant Variety Youth Centre becomes the Project's home on La Grande Route de St Martin — a purpose-built space shared with the wider community.

Registration

Registered Charity No. 340

The Project is formally registered with the Jersey Charity Commissioner and joins the Association of Jersey Charities as member AJC505.

Growing

Juniors, Seniors and the holidays

The weekly timetable expands to separate sessions for younger and older members, and a holiday programme keeps the doors open all year.

Today

Still volunteer-run, still local

Every session is staffed by unpaid volunteers, funded by donations from Islanders and local businesses. There is no head office and no core public funding.
