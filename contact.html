Safeguarding

If a young person is at risk

Maufant Youth Project is run by volunteers who work with children and young people. If you have an immediate concern about the safety or welfare of a young person, contact the States of Jersey Children and Families Hub or the police directly. Our trustees will also respond to any safeguarding concern raised with them.

Maufant Youth Project

A volunteer-run Jersey charity providing youth club sessions and activities for young people aged 9–18 at the Maufant Variety Youth Centre, St Saviour.
