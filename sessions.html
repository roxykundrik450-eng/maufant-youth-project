Sessions & Activities

Somewhere to go, every week

Four regular sessions a week at the Maufant Variety Youth Centre, plus a full holiday programme. All sessions are free for young people aged 9–18.

Term-Time Timetable

What's On This Week

No membership fee and no booking for weekly sessions — just come along. Under-13s are signed in and out by a parent or carer on their first visit.

Tue 16:00

Juniors — Ages 9 to 12

A relaxed after-school session built around play: table games, craft, cooking simple things, and short team challenges. Two adult volunteers on duty at all times.

16:00 – 18:00 Ages 9–12 Free entry No booking needed
Wed 18:30

Seniors — Ages 13 to 18

Music, sport, cooking and group decision-making. Seniors help plan the term's programme and are the ones who represent the Project at Island-wide youth events.

18:30 – 21:00 Ages 13–18 Free entry

Youth Voice — Project Group

For members who want to change something: campaign planning, social action projects, and preparing what to say to parish and Island decision-makers.

17:30 – 19:00 Ages 12–18 Free entry

Open club night. All age groups together, come as you are, bring a friend, stay for as long as you like. The busiest night of the week.

17:00 – 20:00 Ages 9–18 Free entry
What Actually Happens

Inside a Session

Every session has a shape but no script. Here is roughly how the three weekly club nights run — close enough that a nervous first-timer knows what to expect.

Tuesdays · Juniors

The after-school session

Smaller group, shorter evening, more structure. Volunteers run the activity, members run everything else.

  • 16:00  Doors open — tuck counter, table tennis ladder, sign-in
  • 16:30  The planned activity: clay, cooking, printing or a team challenge
  • 17:15  Free time in the hall and the games room
  • 17:45  Everyone helps tidy up, then sign-out
Wednesdays · Seniors

The night the group runs

The longest session of the week, and the one where members make the decisions — including what next week looks like.

  • 18:30  Arrive — the kitchen opens and someone is always cooking
  • 19:00  Band practice, sport in the hall, or the project group in the back room
  • 20:15  Food together, then the group plans the following week
  • 21:00  Close
Fridays · All years

Drop in and see

No fixed programme and no sign-up sheet — the busiest night of the week, and the easiest one to try first. Members are welcome to bring a friend along.

  • 17:00  Doors open to every age group
  • 17:00  Sport, music, kitchen, sofa — whatever the room decides
  • 20:00  Close
School Holidays

The Holiday Programme

When the schools close, the Project stays open. The holiday programme is the single biggest cost we carry all year — and entirely donation-funded.

Day Trips

Beach & Coastal Days

Low tide rockpooling, beach games and a picnic — making the most of living on an island that many of our members rarely get to explore.

Workshops

Skills & Making

Cooking, music, sport and creative workshops led by volunteers and visiting local practitioners — no cost to members.

Residential

Away From Home

A short residential for our older members — often the first time away from home, and always the highlight of the year.

Holiday places are limited

Because trips are funded by donations, places are capped and allocated by booking. Priority is given to members who attend regularly and to families for whom the school holidays are the hardest weeks of the year.

Ask About Holiday Places
Cost Per Place
£0

Free to every member. The average trip costs the Project around £250 per young person in transport, food and staffing.

For Parents & Carers

What to Expect on a First Visit

02
Meet the team

Questions We Get Asked

Parents & Carers FAQ

If your question is not here, ring or email us — there is always someone to talk to.

Does it cost anything?

No. There is no membership fee, no uniform and no equipment to buy, and the holiday programme is free to members as well. Snacks at the tuck counter are priced at cost, and nobody is ever turned away because they have no money that week.

Do we need to book a place?

Not for the weekly club nights — young people can simply turn up. The holiday programme does need booking, because trips are capped by the number of places we can fund and staff. Get in touch and we will add you to the list.

What ages do you take?
  • Juniors — 9 to 12, Tuesday afternoons
  • Seniors — 13 to 18, Wednesday evenings
  • Drop in — all ages 9 to 18, Friday evenings

Under-13s are signed in and out by a parent or carer, and we will not release a junior to anyone who is not on their list.

My child is shy, or has additional needs. How does that work?

Very few young people walk in and join straight away, and that is completely normal. Tell us in advance if it would help and we will arrange for them to meet a volunteer before the session, when the building is quiet. There is a small room off the hall that anyone can use to take a break, and nobody is ever pushed to take part in an activity.

Who are the adults in the room?

Local volunteers — parents, former members, neighbours and trustees. All session volunteers are recruited through a written process, provide references and are checked before they work with young people, and two adults are present at all times. The Project has a named safeguarding lead, and our safeguarding policy is available on request.

Can I stay for the first visit?

Yes, and many parents do. Come in, have a look around, meet whoever is on the door. After that you are welcome to drop off and collect, or to stay and help — some of our longest-serving volunteers started by sitting at the back with a cup of tea.

How do we get to the Centre?

The Maufant Variety Youth Centre sits on La Grande Route de St Martin in St Saviour, JE2 7GT, on the main road with parking on site. If transport is the thing standing in the way, tell us — it is exactly the kind of practical problem we try to solve rather than accept.
